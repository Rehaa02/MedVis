Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Information handled

The workspace handles DICOM files, de-identified study metadata, review decisions, audit records, and operational logs needed to provide the configured clinical workflow.

Use of information

Information is used to ingest studies, render images, produce assistive analysis, support radiologist review, and maintain an auditable record of decisions. It is not used for advertising.

Storage and access

Data is stored in the storage location configured by the deployment administrator. Access should be limited through the organization's identity, network, and retention controls.

De-identification

The ingestion workflow applies the configured DICOM de-identification process. Administrators remain responsible for validating the profile, retention period, access controls, and local privacy obligations before production use.

Questions

For deletion, access, or privacy questions, contact the administrator responsible for this deployment.
