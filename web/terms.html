Terms of Service

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Purpose

ClinAI is a clinical imaging review workspace intended to support qualified healthcare professionals. It does not replace independent clinical judgment, a radiologist's review, or applicable institutional procedures.

Authorized use

Use this service only with authorization from your organization and only for studies you are permitted to access. Keep account credentials and patient information secure. Do not use the service for emergency decisions without appropriate professional oversight.

AI-generated content

Reports, triage results, measurements, and security evaluations are assistive outputs. Review them against the source images and the patient's clinical context before signing or acting on them.

Availability and changes

This local deployment may be changed, interrupted, or taken offline for maintenance. The service is provided for the configured environment and does not promise uninterrupted availability.

Contact

For access, clinical governance, or security questions, contact the administrator responsible for this deployment.
